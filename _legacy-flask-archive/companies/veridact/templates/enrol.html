Enrol a capture device

A device (a camera app, a phone, a recorder) generates a keypair in your browser. Veridact stores only the public key — the private key never leaves the device. From then on, anything this device signs at capture can be verified as genuinely from it, and Veridact can never forge a signature in its name. Enrolling is a one-time step: after this, capturing needs no key handling at all (decisions/0008).
