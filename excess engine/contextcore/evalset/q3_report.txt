Q3 Financial Summary

Revenue grew twelve percent quarter over quarter, driven primarily by new
enterprise contracts signed in August and September. The sales team closed
four new logos in the mid-market segment.

Engineering Update

The authentication system rewrite shipped in October, closing out three
long-standing security tickets. The team also migrated the build pipeline
to a faster CI runner, cutting average build time from eleven minutes to
four.

Customer Success

Churn dropped after the onboarding redesign launched in September, with the
biggest improvement in the first-week retention cohort. Support ticket
volume held steady despite the growth in active accounts.
